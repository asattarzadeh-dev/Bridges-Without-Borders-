Our Mission

"Bridges Without Borders connects Westview High School students with local refugee youth through mentorship, academic support, and community-driven resources. We empower refugee students to succeed in school, build confidence, and feel supported as they navigate their new communities."

Why Organizations Partner With Us

We built this program to meet the standard your students' families deserve.

Privacy-first by design

No student data is shared without guardian consent. Records are kept in a restricted, access-controlled system — never public.

Faculty supervised

Every match and mentor is overseen by a Westview High School faculty advisor, Mr. Ramos.

Trained, vetted mentors

Student mentors complete an onboarding and safety orientation before being matched with any mentee.
